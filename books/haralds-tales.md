My great-granpa's memoir of growing up in Norway and building a life and family in the US. He's a great story teller, and has an incredible recollection of names and people.

I think it was written when he was around 80 years old at my grandma and great aunt's prompting, which makes it even more impressive.

Reading this was super meaningful. There are so many aspects of myself that I feel like I can see directly come from him.
