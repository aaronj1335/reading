This was like the 2nd book I read as an adult and it blew my mind. The prose somehow evokes the jungle of the setting... lush the the point of cloying... but in a way that totally adds to the book. IDK.

Also the jumping back and forth in time so that the climax is chronologically in the middle. Long before Christopher Nolan.
