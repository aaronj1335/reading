Initially this struck me as unimaginative... a writer returning from the big city and career success to her small home town...

But that aspect of returning to home ended up resonating with me. When she realizes that Cal sees them as being on opposite sides and they don't just have a perfect and simple relationship, and even more than that, how Atticus is no longer one dimensionally good.
